☀️ Start of day (at your Mac)

cd ~ claude

Two lines in Terminal. Auto-remote is on, so it's phone-ready instantly. Leave the window open and the Mac awake — that keeps your phone connected all day.

One rule for success: with referrals, give name + address/zip + agency in one shot — "John Doe, 75216, Coker." That lets it check rate, day-rule, and open room all at once. Vague gets follow-up questions; specific gets an instant answer.
